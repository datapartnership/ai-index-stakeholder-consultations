We want to measure AI adoption and characteristics of AI use across the world by combining data from multiple frontier AI labs.

Characteristics of AI use can be measured by classifying user–chatbot conversations according to a defined set of indicators.

For example: Use Case of conversation, Language of Interaction.

Currently, we are considering measuring characteristics of AI use by 6 indicators.

  • Use Case
  • Work Activities
  • Occupational Topic
  • Industry
  • Proficiency
  • Language of Interaction

Indicators, isolated or in combination with each other, may help answer different research questions.

If you are looking for

Are teachers in Rwanda using AI to teach?

You will be able to combine the indicators to answer

Are chats in Rwanda being used for…

  • Use CaseEducation
  • Work Activities
    • Develop educational programs, plans, or procedures
    • Prepare informational or instructional materials
    • Assess student capabilities, needs, or performance
  • Occupational TopicTeaching
  • IndustryP: Education
  • Proficiency
  • Language of Interaction

Another example:

If you are looking for

Are doctors and nurses in Rwanda using AI for diagnosis?

You will be able to combine the indicators to answer

Are chats in Rwanda being used for…

  • Use CaseWork
  • Work Activities
    • Diagnose health conditions or disorders
    • Analyze health or medical data
  • Occupational TopicHealth
  • IndustryQ: Human health and social work
  • Proficiency
    • High
    • Medium
  • Language of Interaction
