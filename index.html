We want to measure AI adoption and characteristics of AI use across the world by combining data from multiple frontier AI labs.

Characteristics of AI use can be measured by classifying user–chatbot conversations according to a defined set of indicators.

For example: the main work activity performed in the chat.

Indicators, isolated or in combination with each other, may help answer different research questions, such as:

Is AI being used to analyse data or information in Rwanda?

Are workers using AI for work or for personal purposes?

We want your input on which indicators are most useful to measure, and what is the best way to measure them.

Taxonomy

For each indicator, review the definition, evaluate its relevance to your work, and share concrete examples of how it may be useful to you. This will help us request these data from the frontier AI labs (Google, Microsoft, Anthropic and OpenAI).

Scope: our current analytical framework focuses specifically on consumer chat data from generative AI platforms.
